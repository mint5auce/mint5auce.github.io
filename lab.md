# Lab

A few personal projects and experiments, built around things I'm curious about.

- [WTHz-D](https://github.com/mint5auce/wthz-d-releases) - A native Mac workspace for FT8 decoding, maps, propagation and spectrum analysis, currently in early alpha.
- [Solar Horizon](https://github.com/mint5auce/solar-horizon) - Animated macOS wallpapers and a screensaver built from NASA's latest solar observations.
- [Phosphor](https://github.com/mint5auce/phosphor) - A completely over-engineered, old-school flight radar that pulls real-time flight data from ADS-B dongles or sources of your choice.
